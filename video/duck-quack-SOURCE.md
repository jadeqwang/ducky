# Real-duck quack source

`duck-quack.wav` is the second call in **“Mallard (Anas platyrhynchos)”**, a
female Mallard recorded by Aubrey John Williams at Rye Grove, Surrey, England,
on 26 January 1976. The recording was provided by the British Library and is
licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

- Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Mallard_(Anas_platyrhynchos)_(W1CDR0001518_BD17).ogg
- Edit: the second quack, excerpted approximately 0.96–1.39 seconds, converted
  to mono WAV, high-pass filtered, level-adjusted, and given short edge fades.
- Use: synchronized to the final head nod in `micro.mp4`.

The edited audio remains available under CC BY-SA 4.0.
